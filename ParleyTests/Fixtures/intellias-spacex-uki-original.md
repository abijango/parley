## Executive Summary

Jason Creane (SpaceX AI), Andre Nedelcoux (Intellias) and Naufal Mir (Intellius) met to turn the SpaceX AI partnership into concrete joint work in the UK/Europe and the US. Jason Creane opened by apologising for the difficulty in scheduling the call. He explained that Khadir, who had been managing the partnership, has left, and that he is now taking ownership. Jason (Monument Hobbies) is listed as an attendee but is not clearly identifiable in the transcript.

The Intellias side said their partner portal sign-up appeared complete, but their name was missing from the partnership launch announcement. Jason Creane agreed to check with SpaceX's global partnership heads. Andre's takeaway from his New York meeting with Scott the previous week was that the partnership should cover both Cursor/agentic development and the Grok API for business transformation.

Jason Creane then described several live opportunities where SpaceX AI needs a delivery and implementation partner: London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG), Experian, Ernst & Young (EY), Shell, HSBC and Barclays. Both Intellias-side attendees chose LSEG and Experian as the priority pair. They know both accounts from previous roles, and both report into the same SpaceX director. The immediate next step is a joint working session the following week, with Pauline, Jacob Anderson, the account leads and field engineers. Jason Creane will schedule it and send an update before the end of the day.

## Key Topics Discussed

### Partner program status and portal confusion
- Naufal said Intellius/Intellias is signed up on the partner portal, based on what Bradley and Christina told him.
- His original portal link expired and he was supposed to receive a new one but did not.
- The company's name was not on the partnership launch announcement or the LinkedIn post, which confused Naufal and Andre.
- Naufal said he wrote the joint venture document himself and sent it over, so the Intellias side believes it has nothing outstanding. He offered to complete anything else that is needed.
- Naufal's theory is that the staff transition caused something to be missed.
- Jason Creane said Khadir left his post, which caused a gap because he had been waiting for Khadir to own and progress the partnership. He is now taking it back and wants no balls dropped.
- Jason Creane will check with Tony Adams and Tony Lee, who head partnerships globally, immediately after the call.

### Partnership scope and operating geography
- Andre met Scott in New York the previous week. The conversation started around Cursor and agentic development.
- Scott's view was that the partnership should also cover the Grok API and business transformation with the models. Andre said Intellias is interested in both approaches.
- Jason Creane summarised the intended operating model as dual: New York/US and EMEA.
- SpaceX AI is entering its fiscal Q4.
- A further conversation is lined up for Monday with Pauline on how to work within the boundaries of SpaceX's "FD" organisation. The transcript is unclear whether this refers to forward-deployed engineering.
- Andre wants a practical approach: find a client to work on together. If it goes well, it becomes the launch for more work.

### Opportunity: London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG)
- Jason Creane said SpaceX AI is close to closing a deal in the very near term, with strong CIO and CTO alignment.
- The project is a large refactoring and heavy infrastructure effort with major cost implications. It is a big-ticket item.
- SpaceX expects the client to need help, and SpaceX will too, in turning the technology into business outcomes. Jason Creane sees Intellias as the "glue", providing subject-matter expertise and practical delivery.
- Stakeholders named:
  - Phil Knight, CIO of the infrastructure business, who is on the hook for making it happen.
  - Ken (surname not recalled), the CTO and a champion.
  - Irvan (also "Irfan"/"Irving" in the transcript), the New York CIO and "the big boss". SpaceX has met him two or three times in the last six weeks and he is providing sponsorship.
- Andre suggested the name might be Andrew Knight, and noted that Ken and Irvan came over from Goldman, bringing a large influx of Goldman people.
- A trial starts next week, with procurement starting in parallel, since the client sees it as a pilot rolling into production.
- SpaceX relies on one field engineer to lead the effort. Jason Creane described this as material, with risk and big upside.
- Andre said the sooner Intellias engages, the sooner it can plan the setup it needs. Jason Creane proposed a full briefing from the account lead and the team director, and aligning on bringing Intellias in early as part of pre-sale.
- Naufal was at LSEG just before the call and is already working with them in other ways. A VP at Intellias, whose name is unclear in the transcript, has worked with LSEG for many years.

### Opportunity: Experian
- Experian is a live RFP on the API side of the business, a Grok voice capability to transform its contact centre.
- Part of the RFP covers how the solution will be delivered. SpaceX lacks the confidence, resources and scope to do it internally, and delivery is not part of its business model, so it needs a partner answer.
- Experian is already a customer and spends a decent amount with SpaceX. There are strong champions and engagement on the Cursor side, and this API work would be a new area.
- Andre said he and Naufal know Experian very well from previous work, and Naufal said Experian wanted something similar a couple of years ago. Andre also referred to a shared AWS-era history, but the transcript on this is garbled.

### Opportunity: Ernst & Young (EY)
- EY is a major consultancy with hundreds of thousands of people and is making a bet on SpaceX.
- The problem is adoption: scaling the capability internally and operationalising it. EY has the technology and the TAM but struggles to make it real.
- Jason Creane is worried because OpenAI has more scale here and is going in to make adoption easy.
- Naufal compared it to a solutions architect (SA) role at AWS: translating client problems into how the product solves them and rolling it out at scale.
- Andre said EY is not really a competitor to Intellias. They are in different leagues: EY is bigger and global, while Intellias is a more specialist, engineering-focused partner. He is happy to have a go.

### Other opportunities mentioned
- **Shell:**
  - SpaceX met the CEO and CIO, who are talking about AI transformation.
  - Shell has some Grok and Starlink and is looking at a vertically integrated capability that drives business change.
  - It is global and complex, and SpaceX is still scratching the surface.
  - A CEO-to-Gwynne Shotwell meeting happened a couple of weeks ago to align SpaceX and Shell, and the work is now starting to kick off.
  - Jason Creane said it is on his priority list.
- **HSBC:** Four proofs of value (trials) are sponsored by the group CIO and group COO, and the client wants to kick off in earnest.
- **Barclays:** The bank wants a voice API project on the contact centre side. The transcript says "block voice API", which is probably a transcription error.
- Naufal said some of these could be kicked off quickly and others later. His view: LSEG definitely, HSBC maybe, Experian definitely.

### Prioritisation and engagement approach
- Andre and Naufal agreed LSEG and Experian are the two to focus on, as both are accounts they know from previous roles.
- Both accounts roll into the same SpaceX director through two different reps. He is deep on both.
- Jason Creane advised making things tangible: pick a problem, engage, and decide to do it, since partner arrangements can be "fluffy".
- Proposed format: a session with the director, the two account leads, the field engineering leader and the two field engineers (FEs) working the accounts. Intellias would develop a point of view and advise on what to do.
- Andre said Intellias brings industry and "organisational" experience, meaning enterprise-level know-how.

### Market context and positioning
- Andre noted that OpenAI and Anthropic (which had just made an announcement a week or so earlier) are investing in big consulting businesses. Great models and solutions are not enough, because delivery in regulated industries with the right security and trust is complicated.
- Jason Creane said he and Andre have worked in the FSI space for multiple decades.
- Naufal said a good product is not an immediate sell. The question is whether customers can use it the way they want within their restrictions, and Intellias has that experience from being in the industry so long.
