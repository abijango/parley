## Executive Summary

Naufal Mir and Andre Nedelcoux of Intellias met Jason Creane of SpaceX AI to turn the Intellias and SpaceX AI partnership into concrete joint work across the US and EMEA. Jason Creane apologised for the difficulty in setting up the call and explained that Kadir Erol, who had been running the partnership, has left the post, so Jason Creane has taken ownership of it back. Naufal Mir raised that Intellias is signed up on the partner portal and has submitted its joint venture document, yet was left off the partnership launch announcement and LinkedIn post. Jason Creane committed to check this with Tony Adams and Tony Lee, who head up partnerships globally.

Andre Nedelcoux shared his takeaway from meeting Scott in New York the previous week: the partnership should cover both Cursor and agentic development and the Grok API for business transformation, and Intellias is interested in both. He asked for a practical starting point, a client to work on together. Jason Creane outlined live opportunities where SpaceX AI needs a delivery partner: LSEG, Experian, Ernst & Young (EY), Shell, HSBC and Barclays.

Naufal Mir and Andre Nedelcoux chose LSEG and Experian as the two priority accounts, as both are accounts Intellias has worked with before and Lucy Willoughby has worked with LSEG for many years. Both accounts roll into the same SpaceX AI director. The immediate next step is a joint working session next week with Pauline, Jacob Anderson, the two account leads and the field engineers. Jason Creane will make the introductions and schedule the session, and Naufal Mir volunteered to come in during his holiday so the session is not delayed. Jason Creane will send an update before the end of the day.

## Key Topics Discussed

### Partner programme status and the launch announcement gap
- Naufal Mir said Intellias is a partner and on the portal, based on what Bradley and Christina told him.
- His original portal link expired and he was due a new link, which he has not received.
- Intellias was not on the partnership launch announcement or the LinkedIn post, which confused Naufal Mir and Andre Nedelcoux.
- Naufal Mir said Intellias has provided everything, including the joint venture document, which he wrote himself and sent over. As far as Intellias is aware nothing is outstanding on its side, and he offered to complete anything else needed.
- Naufal Mir had understood Kadir Erol was sorting out the announcement issue, and suggested something was likely missed in a handover when someone left.
- Jason Creane said Kadir Erol has left the post. Part of the gap was Jason Creane waiting for Kadir Erol to own and move the partnership forward; he has now taken control of it back and wants to make sure no balls are dropped.
- Jason Creane will check with Tony Adams and Tony Lee, who head up partnerships globally, immediately after the call.

### Partnership scope and operating model
- Andre Nedelcoux met Scott in New York the previous week. The conversation started around Cursor and agentic development.
- Scott's view was that the partnership should also focus on the Grok API and business transformation with the models. Andre Nedelcoux said Intellias is very interested in both approaches.
- Jason Creane summarised the intended model as dual operating: New York or US, and EMEA.
- Jason Creane said SpaceX AI is entering its fiscal Q4.
- Andre Nedelcoux said a further conversation is lined up for Monday with Pauline on how Intellias works within the boundaries of SpaceX AI's forward deployed engineering (FDE) organisation.
- Andre Nedelcoux wants a practical start: one client to do something together with, which, if it works well, becomes the launch point for more.

### Opportunity: LSEG
- Jason Creane said SpaceX AI has strong CIO and CTO stakeholder alignment to close a deal in the very near term.
- The work is a big refactoring and heavy infrastructure project with big cost implications; Jason Creane called it a big-ticket item for LSEG.
- It sits on the infrastructure side of LSEG, where there are many legacy systems to refactor.
- Jason Creane expects both LSEG and SpaceX AI to need help turning the technology into business outcomes, and sees Intellias as the glue, providing subject-matter expertise and practical delivery.
- Stakeholders named:
  - Phil Knight, CIO of the infrastructure business, on the hook for making it happen. Andre Nedelcoux, checking LinkedIn during the call, suggested the name is Andrew Knight, and Jason Creane appeared to agree.
  - Ken (surname not recalled by Jason Creane), the CTO and a champion.
  - Irvan, CIO in New York and "the big boss". SpaceX AI has met him at least two or three times in the last six weeks and he is giving the sponsorship needed. Andre Nedelcoux referred to him as Urfan, and another voice on the call suggested Irving.
- Andre Nedelcoux said Ken appears to have come with Urfan from Goldman, part of a large influx of Goldman people Urfan brought in a couple of years ago, while Andrew Knight has been at LSEG longer.
- A trial begins next week, with procurement starting in parallel, as LSEG sees it as a pilot rolling into production.
- SpaceX AI is relying on a single field engineer to lead the effort, which Jason Creane described as material, with risk attached and big upside.
- Jason Creane offered a full briefing from the account lead and the team's director, and alignment on bringing Intellias in early as part of the pre-sale, covering how it would be delivered and what good looks like.
- Andre Nedelcoux said the sooner Intellias is engaged, the sooner it can plan the setup it needs and hit the ground running.
- Naufal Mir had been at LSEG just before the call on a separate matter; Intellias is already working with LSEG in other ways.

### Opportunity: Experian
- A live RFP on the API side of the business, for a Grok voice capability to transform Experian's contact centre.
- Part of the RFP covers how the solution will be delivered. Jason Creane said SpaceX AI lacks the confidence, resources and scope to deliver it internally and delivery is not part of its business model, so it needs an answer to that question.
- Experian is an existing customer that spends a decent amount with SpaceX AI, with strong champions and strong engagement on the Cursor side. The API work would be a new area.
- Andre Nedelcoux said Naufal Mir was the solutions architect (SA) for Experian at AWS for quite a few years, and that he and Naufal Mir have worked with Experian a lot.
- Naufal Mir said Experian wanted Intellias to do something similar a couple of years ago.

### Opportunity: Ernst & Young (EY)
- EY is a big SpaceX AI customer, a major consultancy with hundreds of thousands of people, making a bet on SpaceX AI.
- The issue is scaling the capability internally and operationalising it: EY has the technology and the TAM but is struggling to make it real.
- Jason Creane is worried because OpenAI has much more scale here and is making adoption easy, and enablement and meetings are not the same as developing use cases and wins.
- Naufal Mir framed it as the adoption piece, translating the client's problems into how the product solves them, which he compared to the SA role he and Andre Nedelcoux used to do at AWS.
- Andre Nedelcoux said EY is not really a competitor to Intellias: EY is bigger and global, while Intellias is a more specialist, engineering-focused partner. He was happy to have a go.

### Other opportunities
- **Shell:** SpaceX AI has met the CEO and CIO about AI transformation. Shell has some Grok and Starlink in place and is looking at a vertically integrated capability that drives real business change. Jason Creane called it very meaty, complex and global, with SpaceX AI still scratching the surface. A CEO to Gwynne Shotwell meeting a couple of weeks ago aligned SpaceX and Shell, and the work is now starting to kick off. It is on Jason Creane's priority list.
- **HSBC:** Four proofs of value (trials) sponsored by the group CIO and group COO, and HSBC wants to kick off in earnest.
- **Barclays:** A voice API project on the contact centre side.
- Naufal Mir said some of these could be kicked off quickly and others later, with LSEG definitely, HSBC maybe and Experian definitely.

### Prioritisation and engagement approach
- Andre Nedelcoux said LSEG and Experian are two customers Intellias has worked with in previous roles and knows well. Lucy Willoughby, Intellias VP, has worked with LSEG for many years.
- Andre Nedelcoux proposed a deep dive on LSEG and a one-on-one on Experian, then working out how to drive them together.
- Jason Creane said both accounts roll into the same director through two different reps, and the director is deep on both.
- Jason Creane, recalling Andre Nedelcoux's point from their previous call, said partner arrangements can be fluffy, so the aim is to pick a problem, engage and decide to do it, and he will plug in the right people.
- Proposed format: a session with the director, the two account leads, the field engineering leader and the two field engineers (FEs) working the accounts. Intellias develops a point of view and advises SpaceX AI on what to do.
- Andre Nedelcoux said Intellias brings industry and organisational experience, the enterprise side of delivery.

### Market context and positioning
- Andre Nedelcoux noted that OpenAI and Anthropic (with an announcement a week or two before the call) are investing in big consulting businesses, because great models and solutions are not enough: delivering in a regulated industry with the right security and trust is complicated.
- Naufal Mir said he and Andre Nedelcoux have worked in the FSI space for multiple decades.
- Naufal Mir said a good product is not an immediate sell. Customers need to use it the way they want within the restrictions of their industry, and Intellias has that experience from being in the industry so long.

### Scheduling around Naufal Mir's holiday
- Jason Creane said Pauline is in town and proposed the session for next week, with Pauline, Intellias, Jacob Anderson and the account teams. Jason Creane will join as well.
- Naufal Mir is on holiday next week but volunteered to come into the city for an hour or two rather than delay the session by a week, saying it is something he has been keen to do for a while.
